What areas does Provision Plumbing serve?

We're based in Gulf Shores and serve South Baldwin County, including Orange Beach, Foley, Fort Morgan, Bon Secour, Elberta, Magnolia Springs, Summerdale, Robertsdale, Lillian and Perdido Beach.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Provision Plumbing, LLC is licensed and insured, and owner James Nettles is a Master Plumber. Plumbers in Alabama are regulated by the Alabama Plumbers & Gas Fitters Examining Board.

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Call or send a request through the form and we'll quote the job before any work begins, so there are no surprises on the bill.

Who will actually show up to my house?

Provision is owner-operated. In most cases the owner and Master Plumber, James "Jimmy" Nettles, handles your job himself.

Can you get here the same day?

Often, yes. Same-day service depends on the schedule, so call (251) 709-7180 as early as you can and we'll tell you honestly when we can be there.

Do you work on vacation rentals and condos?

Yes. We work with homeowners, landlords and short-term rental owners across Gulf Shores and Orange Beach. Let us know about access or guest check-in times when you call.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, check and major credit cards.
